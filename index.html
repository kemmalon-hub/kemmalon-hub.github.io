How I work

How a project moves from idea to live.

01Preparation

Use AI language models to quickly summarize competitor research, analyze target audience personas, synthesize project briefs, and generate background knowledge or thematic inspiration.

02Incubation & Ideation

Use generative text and image tools to brainstorm raw concepts, generate quick mood boards, outline initial layout directions, and produce rapid visual variations to break through creative blocks.

03Illumination & Refinement

Leverage AI tools embedded in creative software for asset generation, smart selection, object removal, automated background cleanup, image upscaling, and generative fill to refine components faster.

04Evaluation & Delivery

Use AI to review copy for tone and clarity, automate color harmony checks, adapt visual assets across multiple aspect ratios or formats, and prepare final files efficiently.
