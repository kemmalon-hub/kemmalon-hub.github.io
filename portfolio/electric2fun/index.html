Designing an energetic e-commerce experience for electric personal mobility

Electric2Fun

Electric2Fun is an online retailer specializing in electric scooters, skateboards, hoverboards, and other personal electric vehicles. I designed an e-commerce website that makes these products approachable, highlights their everyday benefits, and gives customers the confidence to explore a newer form of transportation.

Role

UX/UI and e-commerce website design

Category

Web Design

Tools

Figma

The challenge

Personal electric vehicles offer a flexible alternative to conventional transportation, but purchasing one requires more consideration than a typical online transaction.

Potential customers need to understand how products differ, whether they fit their lifestyle, and what kind of support they will receive after making a purchase. Electric2Fun also needed to communicate the enjoyment of riding without overlooking practical concerns such as durability, portability, safety, and maintenance.

The website therefore had to answer several questions quickly:

  • Which electric vehicle is right for me?
  • What makes each model different?
  • Can I easily carry or store it?
  • Is the product reliable and protected by a warranty?
  • Will support be available after my purchase?
  • Why should I choose Electric2Fun over another retailer?

The objective

The goal was to create an engaging shopping experience that balances excitement with product clarity.

The website needed to:

  • Establish a distinctive identity for Electric2Fun.
  • Introduce the featured product immediately.
  • Communicate important product benefits without overwhelming visitors.
  • Make comparing and purchasing vehicles straightforward.
  • Build trust through warranty and customer-support messaging.
  • Educate customers through useful electric-mobility content.
  • Support both direct sales and the company’s affiliate offering.

The solution

The final concept brings commerce, education, and brand storytelling into one connected experience.

The main journey follows a clear progression:

  1. Discover the featured electric vehicle.
  2. Understand its essential benefits.
  3. Learn why the retailer can be trusted.
  4. Browse and compare available products.
  5. Purchase a vehicle or explore the affiliate program.
  6. Continue learning through supporting content.
  7. Understand the people and purpose behind the company.

Global navigation provides direct access to the shop, blog, company information, contact options, account access, and shopping basket.

Outcome

The completed concept gives Electric2Fun an energetic and recognizable online presence while keeping the shopping journey clear.

The design:

  • Communicates the brand’s electric-mobility focus immediately.
  • Makes unfamiliar products easier to understand.
  • Balances emotional product messaging with practical benefits.
  • Establishes trust through warranty and support information.
  • Creates direct paths to shopping and affiliate participation.
  • Supports customer education through an integrated content hub.
  • Gives the company a more personal identity through its About page.

The result is an e-commerce experience designed to make electric personal transportation feel exciting, understandable, and accessible.
